🗓️ Indian General Election Timeline

Understand the complete journey of a Lok Sabha election from announcement to results.

  1. Completed

    Electoral Roll Revision

    Voter list is updated; citizens can register, correct, or delete their entries.

    ~6 months before election

  2. Completed

    Election Announcement

    ECI announces date; Model Code of Conduct (MCC) comes into immediate effect.

    ~2 months before voting

  3. Completed

    Nomination Filing

    Candidates file nomination papers with the Returning Officer of their constituency.

    ~5 weeks before voting

  4. Active Now

    Campaign Period

    Political parties hold rallies, ads air; ECI enforces MCC. Ends 48 hrs before voting.

    3–4 weeks before voting

  5. Upcoming

    Voting Day

    Polls open 7 AM–6 PM. Citizens vote using EVMs; VVPAT provides paper verification.

    Election day

  6. Upcoming

    Vote Counting & Results

    ECI counts EVMs centrally; results announced; winning candidates take oath.

    2–3 days after voting

  7. Upcoming

    Government Formation

    Majority party/coalition invited to form government; PM takes oath at Rashtrapati Bhavan.

    Within weeks of results
